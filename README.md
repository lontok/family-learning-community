# Family Learning Community

Faith sharing resources for married couples, rooted in Ignatian spirituality and Christian Life Community (CLC) traditions.

## Sessions

### 2026-01-22: Showing Up Together

A 90-minute session exploring emotional agility and the Couples Examen.

- [Facilitator Guide](2026-01-22/couples-showing-up-examen.md)
- [Presentation Slides](https://lontok.github.io/family-learning-community/2026-01-22/couples-showing-up-slides.html)

**Theme**: Learning to "show up" to our emotions and each other through Susan David's Emotional Agility framework and Ignatian spirituality.

**Session Flow**:
| Segment | Duration |
|---------|----------|
| Opening Prayer | 5 min |
| Check-In | 40 min |
| Teaching: "Showing Up" | 10 min |
| Couple Breakout #1 | 10 min |
| Couples Examen Introduction | 8 min |
| Couple Breakout #2 | 10 min |
| Group Sharing | 3 min |
| Closing & Scheduling | 4 min |

**Key Takeaway**: The Couples Examen — a 10-15 minute prayer practice for spouses to do together at home.
