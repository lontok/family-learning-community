Showing Up

Emotional Agility

Susan David is a psychologist and author of the bestselling book Emotional Agility. Her TED talk on the topic has been viewed over 10 million times.

"Showing up" means facing into our emotions with curiosity and compassion -rather than avoiding them or being overwhelmed by them.

Two Common Patterns

Bottling

  • Pushing emotions aside
  • "I shouldn't feel this way"
  • "It's not a big deal"
  • Keeping the peace by keeping quiet

Brooding

  • Getting caught up in emotions
  • Ruminating, replaying, stewing
  • "They always do this..."
  • Stuck in resentment or worry

The Alternative

Emotional agility is being flexible with your thoughts and feelings so you can respond to life with intention.

Showing up is turning toward your emotions
with curiosity:

"What am I actually feeling right now?"

"What is this feeling trying to tell me?"

Holding emotions with compassion -
not drowning in them, not dismissing them.

Susan David on Showing Up

Ignatius figured this out 500 years ago.

The Examen is fundamentally
a practice of "showing up."

The Examen Invites Us To:

  • Notice what we're feeling
  • Name the movements of consolation and desolation
  • Approach our inner life with honesty and without judgment
  • Bring it all before God

This is "showing up" - in the Ignatian way.

In Marriage

"Showing up" to another person -
especially the one you share life with most intimately -
requires even more courage.

The Couples Examen

A practice to take home

10-15 minutes • Weekly or Daily • Together

The Couples Examen

Step One
Settle Together
Light a candle if you'd like. Take three slow breaths together.
Acknowledge God's presence with you as a couple.

The Couples Examen

Step Two
Gratitude
Each person shares:
"One thing I'm grateful for today..."

This can be anything -small or large, about your spouse or not.

The Couples Examen

Step Three
Review the Day with Honesty
Each person shares:
"Here's how I really am today..."

Name the emotions present -not to fix them, just to be known.
The listener's only job: receive, not repair.

The Couples Examen

Step Four
Notice the Movements
Each person reflects:

"A moment I felt life/energy today was..." (consolation)

"A moment I felt drained/heavy today was..." (desolation)

The Couples Examen

Step Five
Ask for Grace
Each person prays aloud:
"Lord, help me tomorrow with..."

"Lord, together we look to you. Amen."

10 minutes

Couple Time
